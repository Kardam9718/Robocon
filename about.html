Our Mission

ROBOCON was founded to rethink protective gear for riders who want the best of two worlds: uncompromising safety and forward-thinking luxury design.

Founder — Arjun Rao

Ex-aerospace engineer turned designer. Arjun's vision is to integrate structural safety and clean, futuristic aesthetics — yielding helmets that perform like safety equipment and present like a collector's piece.

Design Principles

  • Structural integrity over trends
  • Minimalism that speaks power
  • Continuous R&D & limited drops
